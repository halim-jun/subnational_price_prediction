## Summary
The merger process is accurately matching most rural and sub-regional administrative units. However, there is a **Critical Granularity Mismatch** affecting major urban centers and capital cities.

## The Problem
The Source Data (Price, Population) often reports data at the **City** or **Region** level (Admin 1), while the Canonical Boundaries are strictly at the **District/Sub-county** level (Admin 2).

As a result, source data labeled with "City" names are not finding a direct match in the "Sub-county" list, and are being dropped.

- **Mombasa**: Similar case. Source has "Mombasa" (market/city). Canonical has sub-counties like `Mvita`, `Changamwe`, `Kisauni`.

### Somalia
